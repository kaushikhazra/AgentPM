## My Recommendation

**For Taskyn, I'd suggest Option 1 (Vibrant Modern)** because:
- The purple primary feels distinctive and modern
- Cyan accents pop nicely for focus states
- Amber/Rose for warnings provide clear visual hierarchy
- Works well in both dark and light modes
- Feels "alive" without being overwhelming

But if you want something easier on the eyes for long coding sessions, **Option 2 (Soft Pastels)** is excellent.
